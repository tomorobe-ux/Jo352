VFB Heritage

Embed from Getty Images

VFB Stuttgart is one of the most succesful football clubs in the Germany, boasting of five Bundesliga titles, four DFB-Pokal titles, one DFL cup and two UEFA Intertoto cup.

The club's roots date back to 1893. Later, in 1912, the local teams Stuttgarter Fußball-Club Cricket 1893 and FV Stuttgart 1893 merged to form Verein für Bewegungsspiele (VfB) Stuttgart, the name the club still proudly carries today.

The club stabilized in the 2020s and had a breakout 2023–24 season under coach Sebastian Hoeneß, finishing second in the Bundesliga — their best league finish since 2007, which earned them a return to the Champions League. They also won the DFB-Pokal in 2024–25, adding to a trophy cabinet that already included several earlier cup wins.

OUR HOME
Embed from Getty Images

Built between 1929 and 1933, The MHP Arena has a capacity of over 60,000.

The stadium hosted games at the 1974 FIFA World Cup, the 1986 European Athletics Championships, the 1988 European Cup final, Euro 1988 fixtures, and the 2006 World Cup. It was also a venue for UEFA Euro 2024, hosting matches including Slovenia vs. Denmark and Germany vs. Hungary.
